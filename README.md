# Asieh Daneshi, PhD

Computer vision and biomedical data scientist in Berlin.

I build image, video and signal-processing pipelines that turn messy experimental data into results people can trust. For six years as a postdoc, first at the University Clinic Bonn and then at the Science of Intelligence cluster (TU Berlin and HU Berlin), I have worked with retinal imaging, EEG and behavioural video data. My PhD is in biomedical engineering.

## Start here

- **[retinal-vessel-segmentation](https://github.com/Asieh-Daneshi/retinal-vessel-segmentation)**: a small PyTorch U-Net for vessel segmentation in retinal fundus images (CHASE_DB1). Split by child, threshold chosen on validation data, Dice 0.80 on unseen children, on par with a second human annotator, plus calibration and error analysis.
- **[imaging-signal-pitfalls](https://github.com/Asieh-Daneshi/imaging-signal-pitfalls)**: small, runnable experiments on things that quietly go wrong in biomedical image and signal processing and model evaluation (spectrograms, filter delay, smoothing, dataset shift with a PyTorch CNN, screening PPV).

## Research code

- **[Offline-Stabilization](https://github.com/Asieh-Daneshi/Offline-Stabilization)** and **[Jitter-Analysis](https://github.com/Asieh-Daneshi/Jitter-Analysis)**: stabilisation of adaptive-optics retinal (AOSLO) videos and measurement of the motion left after stabilisation (University Clinic Bonn, MATLAB).
- **[Offline-Conformity](https://github.com/Asieh-Daneshi/Offline-Conformity)**: VR conformity experiment with eye tracking, data gloves and controllers (Science of Intelligence, Python).
